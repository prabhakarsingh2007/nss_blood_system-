About National Service Scheme (NSS)

The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a public service program sponsored by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India. With the primary focus of "Not Me But You", the objective is to develop students' personalities and character through community services.

At Vidya Vihar Institute of Technology, Purnea, our student-volunteers participate actively in clean campaigns, literacy drives, and medical awareness seminars. The **NSS Emergency Blood Network** stands as our premier welfare initiative to connect community blood requirements with eligible volunteers dynamically.

How the System Works

An organized emergency coordination workflow powered by technology and student volunteers.

1

Register as Donor

Students and staff register as donors, filling in their blood groups and availability.

2

Donor Verification

NSS Coordinators audit profiles and verify contact details before approving them.

3

Blood Request

Patient representatives post emergency blood requests with hospital details.

4

Request Verification

Coordinators verify details with medical authorities to ensure genuine necessity.

5

Donor Matching

The algorithm lists verified donors matching the blood group and location.

6

Donor Contact

The system provides matched donor contacts to verified requesters for coordination.

7

Donation Verification

Following successful donation, the Admin panel audits the record to verify completion.

8

Certificate Issued

A certified, verified NSS appreciation award is issued to the blood hero.

Our Mission

"To make emergency blood support faster, organized and accessible through NSS volunteers and technology."

We believe in community mobilization. By utilizing audited student records and maintaining strict privacy filters, we ensure emergency support remains completely safe, ethical, and instant.

NSS Coordinator / Programme Officer

  • Verify and audit newly registered blood donors.
  • Verify and approve emergency patient requests.
  • Coordinate direct communication for blood supply.
  • Schedule and manage campus blood donation camps.
  • Verify successful donations and issue appreciation certificates.

NSS Volunteers & Donors

  • Register profiles on the network with medical history.
  • Maintain availability toggles and follow 90-day cooldown rules.
  • Participate in scheduled blood donation camps.
  • Donate blood to save lives in critical situations.
  • Download authenticated donation verification certificates.

Privacy & Responsible Blood Support

We prioritize donor privacy and data protection above all. Public directory lists never expose direct donor phone numbers, personal email addresses, or specific residential coordinates.

Matched donor contact coordinates are shared exclusively with patient representatives who have submitted verified, approved blood requests. All patient details remain fully secure and are audited directly by the coordinators to prevent spam or misuse.
