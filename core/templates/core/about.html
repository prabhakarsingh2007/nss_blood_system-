National Service Scheme

Not Me But You:
The Spirit of Community Volunteering

The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a public service program sponsored by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India. The primary objective of NSS is to develop student personality through community service.

At VVIT Purnea, our active NSS unit coordinates a wide variety of social welfare campaigns. The NSS Emergency Blood Network is our core welfare initiative, enabling student-volunteers to stand as active guardians of public health.

Emergency Blood Registry

An Audited, Reliable Registry
of Student & Staff Donors

Unlike public commercial directories, this platform is strictly community-managed. Every blood donor registration is scrutinized by authorized NSS coordinators who verify contact details, campus enrollment, and eligibility metrics (such as the 90-day cooldown period).

When emergencies arise, patient representatives can request blood. The network matches and alerts active donors instantly, ensuring swift coordination and direct support inside the local area of Purnea and wider Bihar.

National Service Scheme (NSS)

VVIT, Purnea, Bihar

This Emergency Blood Network portal is designed, hosted, and audited strictly as a non-commercial, public welfare project by the student-volunteers of the National Service Scheme (NSS) at Vidya Vihar Institute of Technology, Purnea.

Our registry coordinates active volunteer donors within the campus community of students, faculty, and administrative staff. Through direct verification and cooldown audits, we ensure that every donor is healthy, available, and ready to contribute to saving lives.
