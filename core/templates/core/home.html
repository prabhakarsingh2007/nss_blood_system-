An Initiative by National Service Scheme (NSS)

Vidya Vihar Institute of Technology

The NSS Emergency Blood Network is a social welfare initiative coordinated by student volunteers and organizers at Vidya Vihar Institute of Technology (VVIT), Purnea, Bihar. Our mission is to streamline blood availability in critical situations by building a secure, local, and audited directory of active volunteers.

Unlike commercial repositories, this platform is strictly community-led. Every donor in our registry is a registered student volunteer or faculty member whose health parameters, cooling period, and availability are audited by NSS coordinators.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important details to know about blood donation eligibility & processes.

Who is eligible to donate blood?

Any healthy individual aged between 18 and 65 years, weighing at least 45kg, with normal vitals and hemoglobin levels above 12.5 g/dl is eligible to donate. Eligibility is audited online via our "Am I Eligible" checker.

What is the donation cooldown period?

The standard medical cooldown period after a successful donation is 90 days (3 months). Our system locks donors in cooldown automatically to ensure their health and safety.

How are emergency blood requests processed?

Once you submit a request, it enters "Pending Verification". NSS Coordinators verify details with the hospital. Upon approval, matching local donors are assigned and notified automatically via SMS alerts.
